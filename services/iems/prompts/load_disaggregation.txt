## Disaggregation Instructions

Given a sequence of aggregate watt readings at 6-second intervals from a residential sub-panel:

1. Identify which appliances are ON at each time step based on:
   - Power level transitions (step changes indicate appliance ON/OFF events)
   - Characteristic power range for each appliance
   - Typical on-duration and cycling patterns
   - Co-occurrence constraints (e.g., hair drier and water heater both on H2O panel)

2. For the water heater on Panel2:
   - Look for 2000-4000 W bursts lasting 3-8 minutes
   - If solar irradiance is HIGH, expect few or no firings (solar boiler covers demand)
   - Long quiet stretches during sunny weather are NORMAL — do NOT flag as anomalous

3. For the dryer on Shop panel:
   - Sustained 4000-7000 W draw lasting 30-90 minutes
   - Motor cycling creates ~200 W ripple on top of steady draw
   - Largest load in the house — easy to identify

4. For the heat pump on Panel1:
   - Ramps to 1500-4000 W on thermostat call, holds, then drops
   - Below 50°F outdoor temp: expect frequent long cycles
   - Above 60°F outdoor temp: mostly idle

5. For mobile loads (vacuum cleaner):
   - Universal motor signature: ~800-1200 W step lasting 10-30 minutes
   - May appear on ANY panel — output via vacuum_cleaner_status field
   - Distinguished from water heater by duration and steady (non-ramping) draw

Output format: JSON object with one binary array per appliance status field.
