Accuracy, training and data coverage
Research and methods

GPU-trained models are compared with persistence, yesterday and last-week baselines. Model selection uses a chronological validation block; uncertainty calibration and final testing use separate later blocks. A new candidate replaces the champion only after at least 1% better validation error. Replayed observations are excluded from relearning.

TiDE (Das et al., 2023) informs the dense encoder, future-covariate decoder and residual forecast. This is a compact multichannel adaptation. XGBoost (Chen & Guestrin, 2016) provides the CUDA tree model. TimeXer and PatchTST were reviewed as alternatives, not implemented here.

Weather: Open-Meteo GFS previous runs. Historical evaluation uses 48-hour-lead forecasts, avoiding realized future weather. Missing solar readings remain missing. Panel CT polarity is converted to consumed-power magnitude; grid sign is preserved.
